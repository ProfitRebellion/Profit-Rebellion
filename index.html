Questions, answered

Before you get started.

Read these basics and contact us if you need clarification about the software or signal service.

Does the robot guarantee profits?

No. No robot, signal provider or trading strategy can guarantee profits. Forex and gold trading involve significant risk, and you may lose some or all of the money you trade with.

Are the signals on this website live?

No. The dashboard and signal card are illustrative design examples, not live prices or trade calls. Follow the Telegram channel for service updates and verify any signal independently before acting on it.

What platform does the robot work with?

Platform, MetaTrader version, broker server and symbol compatibility must be confirmed before purchase or installation. Do not assume every broker or account type is supported.

Should I use a demo account first?

Yes. Test software on a demo account and understand its behavior, costs and risks before considering a live account. Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose.

How do I purchase or rent the robot?

Contact Profit Rebellion through Telegram to confirm current prices, terms, payment instructions, compatibility and support arrangements. Do not send payment based only on an unverified message.

Is this financial advice?

No. The website, robot and signal information are provided for informational and educational purposes and do not constitute personal investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any instrument.

Risk disclaimer: Trading foreign exchange and precious metals using leverage is high risk and may not be suitable for all investors. Past performance is not indicative of future results. Signals can be wrong, slippage and spreads can affect results, and automation can malfunction or lose money. You are responsible for your own trading decisions.
